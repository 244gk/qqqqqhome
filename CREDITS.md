## Wallpapers (Unsplash)

The built-in wallpapers come from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and are used free of charge under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).
They are cropped to 1920×720, converted to WebP, and stored in `launcher/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`.
Author information was not available when they were downloaded automatically; open the original image links below to find the photographer on Unsplash.

The manifest for online wallpapers (downloaded automatically on Wi-Fi) is `wallpapers/feed.json`; when the Unsplash API is used, the photographer credit is shown in the bottom-right of the launcher.
